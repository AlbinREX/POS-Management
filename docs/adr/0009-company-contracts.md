# ADR 0009: company contracts and B2B account settlement

Phase 9 extends the local NestJS/Prisma authority and the existing Riza frontend. Companies, contracts, authorized employees, the account ledger and statements belong to one organization and branch. No Kosovo fiscalization is introduced.

Company is versioned and soft-archivable. CompanyContract is a versioned DRAFT -> ACTIVE -> SUSPENDED/EXPIRED -> TERMINATED workflow holding the commercial terms: contract number, start/end dates, credit limit, discount percentage, payment terms in days and billing cycle. Only draft contracts may be edited; activation requires an unarchived company and rejects a second active contract, so exactly one contract governs an account at a time. Terminated contracts are final, enforced by a database trigger rather than service code alone. CompanyEmployee records the people authorized to consume on the account; consumption totals are derived from ledger charges rather than stored on the employee.

CompanyLedgerEntry is append-only with a signed amount: CHARGE is positive and references the source order and its payment, PAYMENT is negative, and PAYMENT_REVERSAL is positive and references the original payment. Unlike the Phase 8 supplier ledger, payments are **account-level**: `orderId` and `paymentId` are null on a payment, so one transfer settles a month of orders. Balance is `SUM(amount)` per company and received value is the sum of charges; neither is ever a stored column. A partial unique index permits one charge per order and one case-insensitive payment reference per company.

COMPANY_ACCOUNT is a third PaymentMethod. It settles an order exactly as cash and card do — the existing settlement path remains the only route to PAID — but writes no CashMovement and instead posts the company charge inside the same Serializable transaction as the payment, settlement, inventory consumption, audit and events. The enum value is added by migration; because it is uncommitted inside that transaction, every new-value predicate casts the enum to text, following the Phase 8 StockMovementKind precedent. The `payment_valid` whitelist gains a third disjunct requiring a company and forbidding a shift, tendered cash, change and an external reference. `guard_reversal_insert` and `check_payment_integrity` are replaced rather than left permissive: a house-account payment must have a matching charge and must not have a drawer movement, and a reversal takes neither a shift nor a card reference. A reversal returns credit by appending a negative account entry, never by mutating history.

Credit is enforced twice. The service checks the balance plus the new charge against the active contract limit inside the transaction, and a deferred constraint trigger re-sums at commit as the authority. A zero credit limit means no credit, never unlimited. Only a CHARGE is gated on an active contract and its limit: payments and correction reversals must remain possible after a contract expires or is terminated, otherwise an outstanding balance could never be settled. The trigger also rejects any state where the balance falls below zero, so an account cannot be over-paid.

CompanyStatement and CompanyStatementLine are immutable documents. Generating a statement freezes the period's entries with their order numbers and employee names, records opening balance, charges, payments and closing balance, and derives the due date from the contract's payment terms. A unique constraint on branch, company and period makes generation idempotent, and a deferred trigger requires the stored totals to equal the frozen lines and every line to belong to that company and period. Statements are therefore reproducible evidence rather than a recomputed view that can drift as later entries arrive.

Seven configurable permissions are added: company.read, company.manage, contract.manage, company.charge, company.payment.record, company.payment.reverse and company.statement.manage. Fresh OWNER, ADMIN and MANAGER receive all seven; fresh CASHIER receives only company.read and company.charge, so a cashier can settle an order on account without editing contracts or recording supplier-side settlements. Existing customized roles are preserved; the migration grants and audits only protected OWNER. Collecting on a company account requires company.charge in addition to the existing payment permissions.

Archiving a company requires terminated contracts and a zero balance, so history is never orphaned behind an archive. Composite branch and organization foreign keys bind every reference, including the grandchild path that ties a statement's contract to its company and an employee to their company, making cross-company association structurally impossible. Immutable rows reject update, delete and truncate even under direct database access, and runtime grants are revoked to match.

Out of scope: multi-branch company identity, per-employee spending limits, automated contract expiry, invoice document rendering or delivery, credit notes against a statement, interest or late fees, and fiscalization. An incorrect charge remains auditable and is corrected by an authorized reversal or account payment, never by deleting or rewriting ledger history.
