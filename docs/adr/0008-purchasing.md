# ADR 0008: purchasing and receipt-backed supplier balances

Phase 8 extends the local NestJS/Prisma authority and existing Riza frontend. Supplier records and all purchasing records belong to one organization/branch. No Company Contracts or Fiscalization are introduced.

PurchaseOrder is a versioned DRAFT -> ORDERED -> PARTIALLY_RECEIVED -> RECEIVED workflow. Unreceived drafts/orders may be CANCELLED with a reason. Draft edits retain replaced item rows as voided history; ordered content is frozen. Each line snapshots item name, SKU, stock unit, ordered unit/quantity, unit purchase price and tax rate. Prices are net per selected purchasing unit, up to four decimal places; tax is operator-configured, not inferred from jurisdiction. Totals round half-up per line (net, then tax) to the branch currency's existing two-decimal convention. Receipt amounts use received quantities at the frozen ordered prices. Partial deliveries can consequently differ by a rounding cent from the original estimate.

GoodsReceipt is immutable and belongs to a purchase order. Each receipt requires a supplier delivery/reference, and may record invoice number/date, due date and a safe HTTPS document reference. A supplier reference and any nonempty invoice number must be unique within a branch. One receipt represents one delivery/accounting charge; a single invoice spanning multiple deliveries must be reconciled outside this initial workflow. Posting cannot exceed remaining ordered quantities. Receipt items and positive PURCHASE StockMovements commit in the same Serializable transaction with audit/events, purchase status and a supplier CHARGE entry. Unique receipt-line movement references and command receipts prevent duplicate stock. Existing sales, adjustments and waste keep their ledger contracts unchanged.

SupplierEntry is append-only: CHARGE from a receipt, PAYMENT acknowledging an external supplier payment allocated to one receipt, and a full PAYMENT_REVERSAL referencing an original payment to correct a recording error. Payments are bounded by that receipt's unpaid amount; no unallocated credits/prepayments or currency conversion. They never execute a bank/card transfer or alter POS cash shifts. Supplier outstanding balance is SUM(entries), and received value is SUM(charges). Purchase orders alone do not create a payable. This is an operational balance for recorded receipts, not a certified general ledger.

Receipt posting acquires the branch purchasing revision before inventory revision; inventory operations never acquire purchasing locks. Source links use composite branch foreign keys. Deferred database checks enforce complete receiving, exact quantity/unit/cost calculations, stock sources, status and charges/balances. New mutable identities use optimistic versions; immutable rows reject update/delete/truncate even under direct database access. Durable purchasing/inventory events commit with their records and expose branch bigint cursors. The UI uses LAN refresh and exact retry receipts, not a disconnected write queue.

Fresh OWNER/ADMIN/MANAGER receive supplier.read/manage, purchase.read/manage/receive and supplier.payment.record/reverse. STOREKEEPER receives supplier.read/manage and purchase.read/manage/receive, without supplier settlement authority. Existing customized roles remain intact except audited protected OWNER grants. Catalog pickers expose only necessary item/warehouse metadata under purchase.read.

Receipt returns/credit notes, amendments after ordering, attachments uploads, freight allocation, multi-currency accounting and weighted stock valuation are outside this phase. An incorrect posted receipt remains auditable; do not delete or silently replace its stock or payable history. These limits must be visible in documentation before operational adoption.
