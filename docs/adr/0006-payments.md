# ADR 0006 — Local payment ledger and cash register shifts

Phase 6 only. Accepted 2026-09-12.

The restaurant database remains authoritative. Every collection, reversal and shift command runs in one serializable transaction with an actor-bound UUID command receipt, immutable audit entry and branch event. Money crosses JSON as fixed-scale strings and is calculated in integer cents. Concurrent or stale commands fail with conflict; an uncertain client retries the same command, never invents a second receipt.

Payments are completed local acknowledgements of cash received or an externally approved card-terminal transaction. Card collection and card refunds require a unique external reference. No PAN, CVV, expiry, authorization secrets, online acquiring or simulated gateway is implemented. The cashier must verify the physical card terminal result before recording it. Atomicity with an external terminal cannot be guaranteed without a provider-specific reconciliation protocol.

Collection is allowed only for nonempty WAITING_PAYMENT orders. Submitted orders must already satisfy the existing all-stations-ready rule. First collection creates an immutable OrderBill and freezes the total, items and assignments. AMOUNT mode supports partial and mixed-tender collections. ITEMS mode selects quantities from immutable discounted line totals; integer remainder cents belong to the earliest unit numbers, ensuring that all units sum exactly to the line total. Each payment owns immutable unit allocations. Cancelled payments release their allocations logically. Modes cannot be mixed on one bill. A batch of cash/card tenders commits atomically.

The last valid collection creates an immutable OrderSettlement for exactly the order total, marks the order PAID, and frees its table. Zero-total orders require an explicit settlement command. Database deferred constraints enforce exact settlement, no overcollection, allocation limits and matching cash entries. A PAID order and its financial total never reopen or change. Kitchen READY tickets may still become COMPLETED after settlement without modifying the PAID order.

Before settlement, payment.cancel appends a full cancellation referencing the original payment; the amount due increases and item units become available. A cancelled payment is never deleted. After settlement, payment.refund appends partial or full refunds referencing the original payment, capped at its unreversed amount and using the original method. Refunds do not erase the original settlement or change PAID to unpaid. Refunded totals and net receipts are exposed separately. An order with outstanding receipts cannot be cancelled. An order whose receipts were fully cancelled can be cancelled, but its frozen bill cannot be edited.

Cash shifts belong to one registered active POS terminal and one cashier in a branch. Only one open shift per terminal and per cashier is allowed. Operators can use their own open shift; branch managers with cash.shift.read can inspect history. Opening, cash in, cash out, collection, cash reversal and closing are tracked. Cash change is tendered minus applied amount; the drawer receives only the applied amount. Expected closing cash is opening cash plus immutable signed movements. A count and reason for any difference finalize an immutable close. Refunds against a closed shift use the cashier's current open shift, preserving the original transaction's history. Drawer outflows cannot exceed expected cash.

New configurable permissions: payment.read, payment.collect, payment.cancel, payment.refund, cash.shift.read, cash.shift.manage, cash.movement.manage. Fresh CASHIER gets collection and own shift operations, not cancellation/refund. Fresh ADMIN/MANAGER get all. Upgrades grant new keys only to protected OWNER and audit that change; existing customized roles are preserved.

The existing Next application gains a touch-friendly Payments workspace. The API exposes persisted payment events with string BIGINT cursors, ready for later authorized WebSocket delivery. No Internet-dependent writes, background payment retries, Inventory or Fiscalization are added. Physical terminal identification still relies on authenticated local operators; LAN TLS and managed devices remain deployment requirements.
